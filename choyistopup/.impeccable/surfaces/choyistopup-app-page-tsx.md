## Scope and mode

Public Choyis TopUp home and shared utility checkout. Persuade for immediate trust, then Operate for the transaction.

## Audience and job

Mobile-first Nigerian customers who need to buy airtime/data or pay a utility quickly, including customers who benefit from large readable controls and future voice assistance.

## Primary action

Choose a service and complete its real existing checkout in one page without searching through navigation.

## Proof and content

Live provider catalogue results, existing account verification, internal transaction references, payment reconciliation and recoverable receipts. No invented activity, plans, customer counts or speed claims.

## Constraints

This is an isolated application. It imports existing checkout components without modifying the current TOPUP app or deployed Edge Functions. Provider secrets remain server-side. International Airtime stays unavailable until its secure multi-step catalogue route exists.

## Direction and memorable moment

International payment counter: large Choyis typography, a seven-service custom pictogram grid and one deep-blue checkout workspace that changes in place.

## Open decisions

Guest payment requires a backend identity policy; international airtime requires a new secure catalogue route; future login, transaction tracking and AI/voice entry are not part of this first isolated surface.
